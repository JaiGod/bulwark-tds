How to play

Enemies march along the path toward your Bulwark. Pick a unit from the bottom bar, then click or tap a tile to deploy it. Some units only fit on ground, some only on raised cliffs.

Click a unit to upgrade (4 levels, its look changes), sell, change targeting (First / Last / Strong / Close) and fire its ability (unlocks at level 2).

Hidden enemies shimmer and need detection (Spotter, or upgraded units). Flyers can only be hit by anti-air capable units. Plated enemies shrug off weak hits, Aegis carry shields, Tenders heal and Jammers shut your units down unless a Mender Drone is near.

Mini-bosses arrive on waves 10, 20, 25, 30 and 35. Wave 40 brings The Monolith, a three-phase boss.

Keys: 1-5 pick unit, U upgrade, X sell, T targeting, Q ability, Space start/skip wave, F speed, P pause, Esc cancel.

Every run earns coins and XP. Spend coins on new units and crates of cosmetic skins. Your run is saved between waves.
